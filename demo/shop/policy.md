# Tesla Electronics: Returns, Refunds and Delivery Policy

This document describes how Tesla Electronics handles deliveries, returns, refunds and warranty claims for purchases made on our website or over WhatsApp. It applies to all customers. Where an item's product page states a different return window, the product page applies.

## 1. Order statuses

Every order moves through these stages, and the status you are told is always one of them.

- Packing: the order is being prepared in our warehouse. It has not left the building yet.
- Shipped: the order has been handed to the carrier and is on its way. A tracking link is sent by email and by WhatsApp the moment the carrier collects it.
- Delivered: the carrier has confirmed delivery to the address on the order. The return window starts on this date.
- Cancelled: the order was stopped before shipping and nothing was charged, or the charge has been reversed.

## 2. Delivery times

- Small appliances (kettles, toasters, microwaves, vacuums): 2 to 4 working days after the order is placed.
- Large appliances (washers, dishwashers, refrigerators): 4 to 8 working days, delivered by a two-person team. We phone or message the day before with a delivery slot.
- Large appliances are delivered to the room of your choice on the ground floor. Stairs, installation and removal of an old appliance are separate services chosen at checkout and are not included by default.
- Delivery is free on orders over $500. Below that, a flat $29 delivery fee applies and is shown before you pay.

## 3. Changing or cancelling an order before it ships

- An order that is still Packing can be cancelled free of charge. The cancellation is done by our store team on the website under My Orders, or by asking the store team. A full refund follows within 3 to 5 working days.
- An order that is still Packing cannot be returned, because nothing has been delivered yet. Cancellation is the right route.
- Once an order is Shipped it can no longer be cancelled. You may refuse the delivery at the door, which the carrier records as a refused delivery, or accept it and return it under section 4.
- Changing the delivery address is possible while the order is Packing. After that, contact the carrier with the tracking number.

## 4. Return windows

The return window starts on the day the order is marked Delivered.

- Large appliances (washers, dryers, dishwashers, refrigerators, ranges) and cordless vacuums: 30 days.
- Small appliances (kettles, toasters, blenders, coffee makers) and microwaves: 14 days.
- Clearance and open-box items: 7 days, and only for faults, not for change of mind.

After the window has passed we cannot accept a standard return. A fault that appears later is handled under the manufacturer's warranty, see section 8.

## 5. What can and cannot be returned

Accepted within the window:

- Items that arrived damaged, faulty or not working.
- Items that are not what you ordered.
- Unused items in their original packaging with all accessories, for change of mind.

Not accepted:

- Items that have been installed, plumbed in or hard-wired, unless they are faulty.
- Items with signs of use, missing accessories or missing packaging, for a change-of-mind return.
- Consumables that have been opened: water filters, descaler, cleaning tablets.
- Items marked "final sale" on the product page.

## 6. How to start a return

1. Tell us which item you are returning and why. A photo of the damage or fault helps us approve the return faster and is required for damaged-on-arrival claims.
2. We open a return and give you a return reference beginning with RET. Keep it. Every later message about the return should quote it.
3. Only one return can be open on an order at a time. If you already have an open return on an order, we will give you its status rather than open another.
4. For small appliances, we email a prepaid return label within one working day. Drop the parcel at any carrier point.
5. For large appliances, we arrange a collection. Our team phones or messages within two working days to agree a collection slot. Please have the item disconnected and accessible.

## 7. Refunds and return charges

- A return moves through four stages: Requested, Approved, Received and Refunded. You can ask for the status at any time.
- Once the item is received and checked, the refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 working days. Your bank may take a further 3 to 5 working days to show it.
- Damaged, faulty or wrong items: full refund including any delivery fee. No collection charge.
- Change of mind, small appliances: full refund of the item price. The original delivery fee, if any, is not refunded.
- Change of mind, large appliances: a $49 collection fee is deducted from the refund. Where the item has been unboxed but is otherwise as new, a further 15 percent restocking fee applies. Both are stated when the return is opened.
- Exchanges are handled as a return plus a new order, so the new item ships as soon as the return is Approved rather than waiting for the old one to arrive back.

## 8. Warranty

- Every appliance carries the manufacturer's warranty, normally 12 months for small appliances and 24 months for large appliances, from the delivery date.
- A fault inside the return window is treated as a return under section 4 if you prefer a refund, or as a warranty repair or replacement if you prefer to keep the model.
- A fault after the return window is a warranty claim. We register it with the manufacturer on your behalf and give you their case number. Repairs are carried out in your home for large appliances and by post for small ones.
- Warranty does not cover accidental damage, damage from incorrect installation by a third party, or normal wear.

## 9. Damaged on arrival

- Check large appliances at the door before signing. If the packaging is crushed or torn, write "damaged" on the carrier's device or note when you sign, and photograph it.
- Report any damage within 48 hours of delivery with photos. Reports inside 48 hours with photos are approved automatically.
- Do not install a damaged appliance. Once it is installed we may only be able to offer a repair rather than a replacement.

## 10. Contacting the store team

- WhatsApp and website chat: for order status, starting a return, return status and policy questions.
- Store team by email: for anything the assistant cannot do, including cancellations, address changes after shipping, and warranty repairs.
- Replies within one working day. Weekends and public holidays are not working days.
